# 👥 HR Analytics

📊 **Employee Workforce & Attrition Analysis using Python, SQL & Power BI**

---

## 📊 Project Overview

HR Analytics is an end-to-end data analytics project focused on understanding employee workforce patterns, employee attrition, demographics, departments, job roles, hiring trends, and termination trends.

The project uses **Python, SQL, and Power BI** to clean, analyze, visualize, and transform HR data into meaningful business insights.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze overall workforce size and employee status
- Measure employee termination and attrition rates
- Analyze employee distribution across departments
- Understand workforce demographics
- Analyze employee distribution by gender
- Study employee age distribution
- Identify the most common job titles
- Analyze hiring trends over time
- Analyze termination trends over time
- Compare termination rates across departments
- Compare termination rates across genders
- Calculate average employee age and tenure
- Provide insights that can support HR workforce planning and retention decisions

## 🔎 Key Findings

### 👥 Workforce Overview

| Metric | Value |
|---|---:|
| Total Employees | 22,214 |
| Active Employees | 18,285 |
| Terminated Employees | 3,929 |
| Overall Termination Rate | 17.69% |

### 🏢 Department Analysis

The analysis compares workforce size and termination rates across departments.

The department with the highest termination rate recorded a:

**23.08% termination rate**

This analysis can help HR teams identify departments that may require further investigation into employee retention and workforce conditions.

---

### 💼 Job Title Analysis

The project identifies the most common job titles across the organization.

**Most Common Job Title: Research Assistant II**

Understanding job-title distribution helps provide visibility into workforce composition and role concentration.

---

### 📅 Hiring Trends

Employee hiring trends are analyzed by year to identify changes in recruitment activity and workforce growth.

This can help organizations understand periods of higher or lower hiring activity and support future workforce planning.

---

### 📉 Termination Trends

Termination trends are analyzed over time to identify changes in employee exits.

Monitoring termination patterns can help HR teams investigate periods of increased attrition and develop appropriate retention strategies.
